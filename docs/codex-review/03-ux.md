# Tiny Blacksmith: player experience and UX review

15 October 2026. Reviewed local HEAD **406f26ce2d446d8984e86da0af3846a18ebfedd2**, after reading `02-gameplay.md` completely. Initial tracked tree clean; pre-existing untracked `build-output/` and `docs/codex-review/`. This report is the only authored project deliverable. No gameplay, UI, tests, assets, scenarios or existing documentation changed; no branch operations.

**Diagnosis:** the interface now tells players what exists and when danger arrives. It is less effective at putting competing choices beside each other, keeping their controls near their consequences, and giving a short explanation of what mattered afterward. The remaining problem is information hierarchy and interaction cost more than missing information or an unsuitable visual identity.

Evidence: **O** = observed on an Android emulator during this review; **S** = verified in current source; **H** = design hypothesis, requiring player validation. Recommendations and benefit estimates are H even when their motivating problem is O/S. No human playtest was conducted. References use `U/` = `app/src/main/java/com/example/blacksmithproject/ui/`, `C/` = `core/src/main/kotlin/com/tinyblacksmith/core/`; line numbers refer to HEAD above. Effort: low = local presentation change; medium = several UI models/interactions; high = gameplay or persistence work.

## A. Five problems that matter most

1. **The daily priority can promote administration ahead of the strategic situation. [O,S]** In the classic `wall_death` scenario, “Worth doing first” is a commission due in two days while tonight's siege has dire odds. `C/shopday/Advice.kt:25` uses a fixed priority order: urgent orders, empty shelf and price refusals precede defence. The siege header itself works. The mismatch is between a strong recommendation and the larger situation. Prefer an honest optional suggestion, with a siege-relevant action beside it when warranted. Do not make an empty shelf or unused energy read as unfinished homework.

2. **The Forge makes comparison a sequence of edits and reading. [S]** Ingredient selection closes its tray immediately (`U/ForgePanel.kt:164`); effect descriptions sit below its option grid (`:351`). The combined outlook then repeats parts, prerequisites, tradeoffs and chance effects (`:432`). Players must retain the old combination mentally. Keep alternatives and one selected effect together; show the change to the assembled recipe before closing the tray. A workbench should support trying a thought, seeing its consequence, then committing.

3. **Guild navigation improved, but choices still live far apart. [O,S]** Today, sub-tabs and compact offers are implemented. In the observed siege-day Contracts view, the pinned reminder says “No contract planned today” and only one offer fits below the board heading. Wall, equipment and contract decisions require separate views. The party sheet's confirmation follows the entire roster, retreat rules and quest stages (`U/ContractSheet.kt:76`). Improve comparison and confirmation within these existing parts; do not replace the remaster or imply sabotage is compulsory.

4. **The climax repeats facts before exposing their useful explanation. [O,S]** The observed breach card repeats 12 damage in its receipt, notes and narrative; a Charge event appears in highlights and prose. Named weapon accounting already exists in the battle report. Put one recorded weapon/hero contribution near the outcome, then one compact consequence receipt, then detail. Attribution must distinguish contribution from proof that something caused victory or defeat.

5. **Persistent chrome and repeated framing consume the space needed for decisions. [O,S]** At 1.5× text, the Guild reminder truncates “No contract planned tod…”; the Wall initially exposes threat identity and health, with defenders and counters farther down. The fonts and colours are readable. Shorten repeated content and reflow action rows before shrinking text. Reduce ornament around routine information while retaining art and ceremony at the anvil and siege outcome.

## B. Screen-by-screen findings

### Shop and the daily decision

**Priority and follow-through. [O,S; medium]** `C/shopday/Advice.kt:25`, `U/ShopUi.kt:42`, `U/WorkshopScreen.kt:160`. The lead contains a hero/commission in core, but `LeadUi` does not retain those IDs. The commission-forging lead falls through to selecting Forge; a want passes only the weapon family. By contrast, the board's “Forge this” uses `vm.forgeFor` (`WorkshopScreen.kt:237`). A named recommendation can therefore lose its purpose at the next tap. Carry the existing target ID through the lead and reuse the existing commission path; for wants, retain the named budget/power context without implying acceptance or guaranteed purchase. Give a siege-day recommendation the correct mode-specific readiness source; do not use a classic scalar outlook as a Guild battle forecast. Changing the shared advice policy itself belongs in the reconciled roadmap, not an incidental UI patch.

**Opportunities and obligations blur. [O,S; low]** `U/ShopPanel.kt:110`, `:142`, `:154`; `U/WorkshopScreen.kt:405`. The lead, visitor and commissions precede the shelf, while a second board doorway and an always-rendered buying section follow it. New offers are placed before accepted work in `BoardUi.onShop` (`U/CommissionBoard.kt:73`). Present accepted work due soon first, then a compact optional-offer/visitor row. Reuse the existing board; omit its duplicate doorway when its header is already visible, preserving access to wants through that header. Collapse buyer detail once a specific purpose is selected. End Day stays visible, with actionable warnings outside a compact button rather than multiple paragraphs inside it.

**Surplus should be interpreted before it is sorted. [S; medium]** `U/StorageSheet.kt:56`, `:136`, `:244`; `U/Dialogs.kt:198`. Filters, multi-selection, salvage, donation and permanent scrap already exist. Adding bulk cleanup again would miss the problem. Put existing matching stock beside a named commission/want before “Forge this”; distinguish “fits”, “affordable” and “may buy”. The result already makes “Store for commission” primary when this exact weapon is selected for handover (`Dialogs.kt:205`); preserve that. For free experiments, avoid implying that suggested-price listing is evidence of demand. UI can discourage purposeless replacement crafts, but cannot create buyers or fix the measured demand saturation.

### Forge, discovery and practice bench

**Compare in place. [S; medium]** `U/ForgePanel.kt:153`, `:164`, `:341`, `:351`, `:432`. Usable choices commit and close; inspect-only state is available for blocked options. Keep a tray open while trying usable materials, show only the focused material's description and a compact combined change (role, unmet requirement, cost), then Done. Do not list every alternative's full rules below the grid. Preserve stock counts, known affinity marks, blocked reasons and free experimentation. Add no universal “best recipe” score.

**Put controls before their assessment. [S; low]** `U/ForgePanel.kt:167` renders notes/outlook before Quick/Advanced and risk (`:169`, `:490`). Move mode above ingredient slots, with Advanced revealing catalyst/technique choices; place risk by the cost/action. Keep the pinned Forge CTA and contextual Restock (`:542`). At enlarged text, stack mode and risk: the current options row contains a fixed 132dp risk control (`:507`). Actual clipping of that row was not verified.

**Separate uncertainty from missing knowledge. [S; low–medium]** `U/RecordsUi.kt:38`, `:48`; `U/InfoPanels.kt:317`; `U/ForgeUi.kt:323`. The clue ladder labels all four rungs, but its “Use this recipe” action requires actual discovery. Fully earned clues do not guarantee a first signature, as established in `02-gameplay.md`. Show two distinct states: clues complete / signature not yet forged. In the current eligible draft, use the project's verbal chance bands (`ForgeUi.kt:106`), rather than revealing hidden numeric odds or implying a progress guarantee. A miss should say whether a clue changed or the eligible attempt simply failed. Bounding repeated failures is a separate gameplay proposal, not a UI fix.

**Reduce repeat teaching in the result. [S; low]** `U/Dialogs.kt:174`, `:182`, `:184`, `:223`. The rarity/property tutorial is rendered on every fresh result before learning and recipe context. Default to the new finding, weapon identity and destination decision; move reusable teaching into existing details. Retain the short skippable reveal, live actions, commission handover truth and large-font scrolling fallback. No longer reveal or extra animation is needed.

**Bench truthfulness is already strong. [S; medium]** `U/BenchSheet.kt:74`, `:109`, `:116`, `:122`; `U/Notebook.kt:144`. The synthetic setup is explicitly disclosed; fired, silent and untested rules are separated; learned/suggested/witnessed notes are distinct. Keep those distinctions. Compact the setup into labelled rows and surface one prerequisite → trigger → measured effect chain for the focused rule, with other chains folded. Do not call this the player's actual encounter or treat an enabled interaction as guaranteed: party order can consume a Mark before the intended wielder acts. The proposed comparison flow needs validation; bench interaction was not exercised on this emulator.

### Guild: Contracts, Company, Wall and Charter

**Contracts need common comparison facts, not more prose. [O,S; medium]** `U/GuildParts.kt:120`, `U/GuildUi.kt` (offer/contract models), `U/ContractSheet.kt:77`, `:128`, `:140`. Danger, duration, fee, rewards and a missed-siege warning already appear. Keep them, but align the same facts in the same order: fee, return day, danger, reward, siege availability. Put return timing on every offer, not only in an opened party sheet. Show exact current repair/preparation effects beside alternatives only when those jobs exist. A Hunt's income or an opportunity's materials are valid alternatives; do not advertise hypothetical counter-leverage from the gameplay proposal as implemented. Move Yesterday below today's board. In the party sheet, keep confirmation plus departure/return and siege warning visible while roster/details scroll; rename “Send the party” to “Plan departure” since leaving happens at End Day.

**Order and equipment are real decisions with expensive access. [S; medium]** `U/ContractSheet.kt:90`, `:100`, `:111`; `U/GuildParts.kt:181`; `U/detail/ItemDetailSheet.kt:387`; `U/detail/HeroDetailSheet.kt:235`. Order is currently the sequence of checkbox selection. Provide move-up/down controls on the chosen members, preserving existing action order and posture semantics. Offer access to the existing hero/loan UI from selection, carrying the draft party back afterward. Keep one status chip, recruitment costs and reserve/unavailability reasons; do not expand the entire company into an equipment dashboard. Validate selection and cancellation continuity across sheets.

**Wall: threat is visible; counters are distant. [O,S; medium]** `U/SiegeHeader.kt:111`, `:161`; `U/GuildParts.kt:217`, `:247`, `:277`. The observed Wall repeats the header's besieger and tonight line, then health/watch and defender rows, before preparation answers. At 1.5× text, even the reminder truncates (`U/GuildPanel.kt:170`). Keep the header as time authority; compress Wall identity to the relevant matchup/preparation, surface the most important availability gap and answers before the full roster. Turn existing preparation answers into links to the appropriate equipment/contract control where resolvable; preserve text when no exact target exists. Full defender count means places filled, not victory assured or correct counters equipped. Keep unavailable defenders and battlefield expanded near siege night as already implemented.

**Today should permit deliberately doing nothing. [O,S; low]** `U/GuildPlanUi.kt:120`, `U/GuildPanel.kt:161`. “No contract planned today” remains a top action on siege night even with all places filled. Label it as an optional opportunity, rather than an outstanding duty, and let urgent readiness/rescue/checkpoint decisions outrank it. Use a vertical task/action layout at large text, and move expanded reminders into the scrolling content so they cannot consume the working area. Do not remove their direct links or daily sub-tab memory. Charter milestones, deep-road unlocks, retirement and rank consequences already exist (`GuildPlanUi.kt:185`, `GuildParts.kt:314`); preserve them. Stronger post-day-20 decisions require gameplay work.

### Daily results, siege replay, Gazette and histories

**One outcome, one receipt, one explanation. [O,S; low–medium]** `U/shopday/DayCards.kt:222` (`SiegeOutcomeCard`), `:247` (notes), `:252` (narrative); `U/replay/SiegeReport.kt:73`, `:95`, `:105`. Observed breach: damage is repeated three times and a Charge sequence twice. Prefer outcome → one salient recorded contribution/preparation fact → forge health/damage and actual casualties → relevant next action. Keep all raw text in the report/Gazette disclosure. Do not deduplicate by fragile string matching; choose which typed fields own each fact. “Down” must remain distinct from death, and damage taken distinct from health now (observed −12 and 90, not an inferred subtraction).

**The report is useful but requires traversal to its explanation. [O,S; medium]** `U/replay/SiegeReport.kt:95`, `:105`, `:144`. Named defenders and blades have totals, expandable rule shares and shared accounting. Promote a selected existing observation above all defender totals, rather than inventing a new attribution engine. In the observed report, Petra's Stormcharged Bronze Axe dealt 10 Arc damage in five activations. That supports “your weapon contributed”, not “this weapon would have won the siege”. Keep unknown/shared contributions honestly unattributed. Add contextual access to the existing hero, weapon or Wall view after the day is acknowledged; respect the historical day while replay is open.

**Replay detail can exceed its readable stage. [S; low–medium]** `U/replay/SiegeReplay.kt:198`, `:310`, `:340`, `:376`, `:395`. Names, pop notes and recent events truncate; the inspector and full report already provide detail. Let a tapped actor/event retain a readable full name and rule chain when paused. Keep short/full replay, manual controls and reduced-motion behaviour. Replay playback, timing comprehension and TalkBack were not tested here; there is no evidence to justify a new replay or more decorative effects.

**Gazette is not an unranked event dump. [S; low–medium]** `C/gazette/Digest.kt:30`, `:40`, `:96`, `:125`; `U/Dialogs.kt:307`; `U/InfoPanels.kt:338`. It already has a five-story default cap with mandatory exceptions, typed event folding, minor-change disclosure and forward notices. Preserve this work. Remaining gap: `StoryBlock` makes only notebook-related stories actionable (`Dialogs.kt:323`), despite stories carrying subject IDs. Reuse those IDs for named hero/blade access where the save or snapshot supports it; do not add another summary page or fabricate missing historical context. Keep “All details” as the archive of record.

**Histories need selective emphasis. [S; low]** `U/detail/ItemDetailSheet.kt:370`, `:376`; `U/detail/HeroDetailSheet.kt:130`, `:215`; `U/RecordsPanel.kt:36`. Weapons show curated Story followed by full History; heroes show shop dealings then up to 12 recent events. Lead with the newest consequential named event, then fold the exhaustive lists, preserving date and provenance. Keep guild townsfolk in Records and classic people in Town. Empty states already exist; shorten established-state guidance rather than adding tutorial paragraphs to every empty panel.

### Readability and premium presentation

**Keep the visual identity; spend less space repeating it. [O,S; low]** `U/theme/Type.kt:30`, `U/theme/Color.kt:5`, `U/ForgePanel.kt:404`, `:428`. Alegreya headings, Fira Sans body, warm charcoal, ivory, brass and pixel art form a coherent fantasy workshop. Body is 15–17sp with generous line height; ingredient names use 14sp labels while surrounding explanation can be larger. Promote choice identity after removing duplicate prose. Reserve filled gold for the main action, clear gold selection for the current choice, and quieter cream labels for informational headings. Use simple rows/dividers for routine facts instead of repeated framed containers. Retain substantial art at the anvil and siege, but reduce stage art before hiding consequential text at enlarged sizes. Do not substitute a generic Material dashboard or another decorative border system.

At 1.5×, the observed siege outcome initially shows the result, receipt and most defender names, while the causal information remains below the fold; its art and controls retain substantial space. The Guild tabs correctly scroll horizontally at this size; a partially visible Charter tab is not itself broken text. Today truncation is observed, but Forge options' fixed-width risks, the 1.0–1.3 tab breakpoint and 2.0× layout remain unverified. Palette comments claim high contrast; this review did not perform a full rendered contrast/accessibility certification. Keep labels and signs alongside colour, minimum hit areas, scalable text and reduced motion. Use plain game terms consistently: power, quality, Guard, Charge, return day; explain a term at its decision point, not in repeated paragraphs.

## C. Simplification audit

| Treatment | Existing content/actions | Implementation intent |
|---|---|---|
| **KEEP** | Shared siege timing; named commissions; free Forge experimentation; stock counts; conditional outlook; synthetic bench warning; Guild parts; exact return/unavailability; replay contributions; typed Gazette; weapon provenance | Preserve agency, uncertainty and truthful records. |
| **SIMPLIFY** | Lead; End Day supporting text; ingredient tray; contract summary; Wall head; result receipt | One decision with nearby consequence, not several headings explaining the same fact. |
| **HIDE until relevant** | Full ingredient rule wording; rarity teaching after first exposure; all bench chains; full histories; nonurgent buyer detail | Reuse current disclosures; never conceal deadlines, fees, material costs, overwork or actual departure warnings. |
| **MOVE** | Quick/Advanced before slots; contract confirmation out of long scroller; return date onto offer; preparation answers above full defender list; battle observation above totals; Yesterday below board | Reduce scrolling and working-memory demands without adding screens. |
| **REMOVE** | Duplicate commission doorway when redundant; repeated siege damage/Charge prose; routine decorative framing; obligation-like language for optional jobs | Remove duplicate presentation, not recorded events, free choices or actual warnings. |

UI-only simplification cannot eliminate classic surplus, make repair a charter-winning strategy, guarantee first signatures or give rank escalation new objectives. Those remain explicitly separate gameplay decisions.

## D. Three proposed layouts

Illustrative hierarchy only. Bracketed values come from current state/record; these are not new predictions or authored outcomes.

### 1. Forge: choose a purpose, compare a material

```text
[Existing day / siege header]
For [named accepted commission]   [Change / Experiment freely]
[existing fit requirements; matching stock if any]

Quick | Advanced             [workbench art, reduced while tray open]
[Weapon] [Metal] [Augment]   [Advanced slots when applicable]

METAL                         [Done]
[available material alternatives, stock and known marks]
[focused material]: [one role / requirement]
Recipe change: [cost change] · [changed effect or unmet enabler]

[Combined role] · [suited classes]        [Details / Practice fight]
[Field notes: pairing stages]            [Notebook]
---------------------------------------------------------------
[Risk]  [cost / overwork / blocker]       [Forge]
```

No hidden signature information or guaranteed combat outcome is exposed. Existing chance effects stay separate from certain rules. Free experiments have no invented customer promise.

### 2. Guild: keep the remaster, bring the decision forward

```text
[Existing day / siege header]
[Only urgent Today item, if present]      [direct action]
Contracts | Company | Wall | Charter

WALL: [preparation] · [matchup]
[filled places] · [actual readiness gap]   [Assign / equipment]
[existing answers to preparation]         [relevant existing controls]
[Forge health] · [Watch]                  [details]
[Defenders / battlefield / missing members, expandable]

CONTRACTS: compare currently available offers
Job     Fee   Back day   Danger   Reward / preparation effect
[offer] [fee] [date]     [word]   [recorded reward]   [Plan]
[missed siege warning beside the affected offer]
[Yesterday, collapsed]   [What comes next, collapsed]

PARTY SHEET: [selected heroes with up/down] [roster below]
[Retreat rule] [Works together / Watch out] [Quest details]
Pinned: [fee · departure · return · siege absence] [Plan departure]
```

Wall and Contracts remain existing sub-tabs, not simultaneous duplicate dashboards. No sabotage default, guaranteed victory gauge or invented repair reserve.

### 3. Siege payoff: meaning before transcript

```text
[Small siege art / day]
[Recorded verdict]
[One recorded preparation or named weapon contribution]

Forge damage [n] · Health now [n]
Fell [names / nobody] · Injured [recorded names]

[Battle report] [Watch siege]
[More moments and original narration, collapsed]
-----------------------------------------------------
[Next / acknowledge day]
Next morning: [relevant existing Wall / hero / weapon action]
```

Ordering is **Outcome → Why it happened → What changed → What next**. “Why” uses a verified observation, explicitly weaker than a counterfactual explanation when that is all the record supports. If no useful attribution exists, omit it. Never turn “went down” into a casualty or synthesize a revenge story.

## F. Gameplay alignment and validation

| Most important UI change | Weakness established in `02-gameplay.md` | What improves, and what remains |
|---|---|---|
| In-place Forge comparison | Conditional rules and party order obscure expensive ingredients' value | Exposes enablers and tradeoffs with less reading. Does not change Frost/Sun coverage, trigger order or classic combat vocabulary. |
| Named purpose carried through Forge, existing stock first | Classic production exceeds demand; service-to-field connection is indirect | Makes an existing job answerable without unnecessary production. Buyer choice/equipping stays autonomous; no guaranteed sale or field use. |
| One truthful siege contribution beside outcome | Forging, heroes and victories can feel disconnected | Makes actual named weapon use visible. Does not invent causality, use or future success. |
| Readiness and counters before repeated Wall summary | Preparation dominates Guild choices; repair may become maintenance | Shows existing answers and availability together. Cannot make weaker alternatives strategically equal. |
| Comparable offers and persistent party confirmation | One party and short mission windows narrow strategic choices | Exposes opportunity cost, return timing and deliberate staying home. Sabotage remains one valid route; new leverage is a separate balance decision. |

Validate the presentation changes with a small formative session before claiming greater engagement: can players identify today's chosen purpose, distinguish an offer from an obligation, find already-fitting stock, explain one conditional recipe effect, predict a party's return relative to the siege, and name one *recorded* contribution afterward? Measure task completion, taps/backtracking and explanation accuracy, comparing current and proposed flows. Include classic and Guild, unscouted and siege-night states, a fully clued undiscovered signature, and normal/enlarged text. Test whether players understand that filling all defender places is not a victory forecast. Do not score success by forcing every player to craft, deploy or sabotage.
