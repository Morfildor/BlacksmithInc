Tree reviewed: `main` at a2e0392 (0.8.0 plus the unreleased run variety, deep forging and guided first day).
Ground truth run today: `:core:test` 622 passed, `:app:testDebugUnitTest` 246 passed, `:app:assembleRelease` and
`:app:bundleRelease` green, instrumented suite 75 run / 8 failed (all stale assertions, no defect), debug APK walked on a
fresh emulator through a guild run and a classic run (screens in `docs/review_2026-10-14/device/`).

Thirteen lenses, each with its own appendix under `docs/review_2026-10-14/` (01 GDD alignment, 02 core code, 03 architecture,
04 Compose and Android, 05 UI design, 06 gameplay and content, 07 compliance and performance, 08 guidance and copy,

## 1. The ten things that matter most

1. **Store identity is untouched**: template application ID, no release signing, template launcher icon (07 B1–B3). Owner work, not code.
2. **A long run locks itself out**: the save row passes SQLite's 2 MB cursor window around day 350–490 and the next launch cannot load or clear it (07 B4, 03#1). Unconfirmed on a device; one instrumented test settles it.
3. **Text reads thin because every body style is Regular weight** and the most used style is 14 sp muted (05#2, 13#2). A token change fixes every screen.
4. **The opening chooser is prose, not a comparison**: three packages, each taller than a screen, three gold buttons, then a stock dialog (05#5, 13#1).
5. **Chrome eats the Shop and Forge**: at font scale 1.0 the lead card is pushed to the fold; at 2.0 only the intro strip and End Day remain (05#13, 04#6, 13#3).
6. **Hidden odds printed as percentages on the workbench** break a LOCKED disclosure rule (01#1, 08#12). Owner decision, not a silent fix.
7. **Price is a cliff**: the expensive band costs ten days of run length, the cheap band only gold, so "List at suggested" is the whole decision (06#1).
8. **A classic run ends by arithmetic after day 10** and a new account never sees a Legendary (06#2, 06#4).
9. **The guided first day never says heroes fight and die**, and a first guild run gets no guild guidance (08#1, 08#3).
10. **`main` is 12 commits ahead of GitHub, CI never ran on it, and 8 device tests are stale** (09#1, 09#2, 11#2).

## 4. Owner decisions

Nothing below is changed without the owner's word. One line each, trade-off included.

- **A. Odds on the workbench.** Keep the percentages (plannable Heartfire) or word them as counts and keep the LOCKED mystery rule. Recommendation: counts, because the repo's own convention and `LaunchContentTest` already say so.
- **B. Guild wall work.** Accept a free +5 a day on top of the classic cap of 2 as the hero-driven repair, or cap it by `maxIntegrityRecoveryPerDay` / a cooldown.
- **C. Debug entries.** Stay on the title (every capture looks like a debug build) or move under the gear (one tap more for testers, `scenarios.sh` updated).
- **D. Large screens.** Declare the app a game (one manifest line, portrait stays locked) or budget a landscape pass.
- **E. Screenshots in git.** Keep tracking evidence PNGs (135 MB now) or move them to LFS or a release zip.
- **F. Version.** Ship 0.9.0 with run-variety gates G3, G12, G13, G14 as known issues, or hold for the phone pass the ledger's R3 asks for.
- **G. Backup.** Keep `allowBackup="false"` (delete the inert rule files) or enable Auto Backup for the legacy only.
- **H. Font.** Bundle a sans at 500/700 and a serif bold (about 1.5 MB, OFL) or stay on the device's Roboto at Medium.
- **I. Opening confirm.** Keep the dialog once the card states the drawback in Body, or drop it.
- **J. Members at home** train and pursue ambitions without a command: accept and narrow the CLAUDE.md sentence, or make the home day a choice.
- **K. Pricing control.** Keep the stepper on the result card, or make "List at suggested" the only control there with manual pricing on the sheet.
- **M. Free listings.** Should a weapon given away at 0 gold earn the same reputation and loyalty as one sold? Trade-off: a generous-smith style against a measured survival edge over fair pricing.
- **L. Forge result for a commission** no longer says whether a weapon is reserved: keep it dropped (delete the stale assertion) or restore one line.
- Still open from the ledgers, with 06's recommendations: synergy band 40–67, drawbacks as a standing cost, militia paid as a wall unit, a Heartfire confirm step, "Renown" kept.
