   (`Stories.kt:84`, `SiegeFight.kt:211`); `GameEngine`/`Forge` hold the "few" CLAUDE.md names plus one it
   does not (`GameEngine.kt:360`, donate +1 reputation).

   gold-free, so LOCKED holds, but it bypasses the classic daily recovery cap (`GameEngine.kt:487`). Owner decision.

   members still rest/train automatically (`GuildOps.kt:309-317`) and have ambitions checked (`Heroes.kt:262`). Low.

   all recorded in DECISIONS; the GDD appendix is not updated, which is fine by its own rule, but the checklist wording over-claims.
