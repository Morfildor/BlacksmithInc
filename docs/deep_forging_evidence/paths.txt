Healing into Charge into a burst  [charge]
Relationship: Anything that stores Charge builds toward one burst at 3. Healing fills a salted weapon, hits fill a Stormcharged one, silver starts two ahead.
Trade-off: The burst waits for the third Charge. A salted weapon stores nothing from healing its wielder does not need.
Another approach: With no healer and no quench, a plain storm weapon arcs every round for less but never waits.

  First recipe: Iron + Stormglass + Binding Salt sword, a Guardian, with a healer
    Against a long fight. WON after 6 rounds, enemy health left 0/120, party health left 82/82.
    FIRED Iron holds x1: Round 1. > Bench Guardian's Iron holds gives Bench Guardian 3 Guard.
    FIRED Arc x7: Bench Guardian: Hold the line. > Bench Guardian hits Ashclaw brute for 12. > Bench Guardian's Arc hits Ashclaw warchief for 2.
    FIRED Capacitor x3: Bench Warden: Mend. > Bench Warden heals Bench Guardian for 10. > Bench Guardian's Capacitor stores Charge (1).
    FIRED Charge burst x2: Bench Warden: Mend. > Bench Warden heals Bench Guardian for 1. > Bench Guardian's Capacitor stores Charge (3). > Bench Guardian's Charge burst spends 3 Charge.

  Second recipe: Silver + Stormglass sword, quenched for Stormcharged, a Duelist, alone
    Against an armoured line. LOST after 5 rounds, enemy health left 5/94, party health left 0/34.
    FIRED Silver holds a charge x1: Round 1. > Bench Duelist's Silver holds a charge stores Charge (2).
    FIRED Arc x5: Bench Duelist: Double cut. > Bench Duelist hits Cinder-knight for 9. > Bench Duelist's Arc hits Bone warden for 2.
    FIRED Stormcharged x5: Bench Duelist: Double cut. > Bench Duelist hits Cinder-knight for 9. > Bench Duelist's Stormcharged stores Charge (3).
    FIRED Charge burst x4: Bench Duelist: Double cut. > Bench Duelist hits Cinder-knight for 9. > Bench Duelist's Stormcharged stores Charge (3). > Bench Duelist's Charge burst spends 3 Charge.

  Without its enabler: The first recipe with nobody to heal
    Against a long fight. TIMED_OUT after 8 rounds, enemy health left 10/120, party health left 16/46.
    FIRED Iron holds x1: Round 1. > Bench Guardian's Iron holds gives Bench Guardian 3 Guard.
    FIRED Arc x8: Bench Guardian: Hold the line. > Bench Guardian hits Ashclaw brute for 12. > Bench Guardian's Arc hits Ashclaw warchief for 2.
    SILENT Capacitor: Nobody healed Bench Guardian.
    SILENT Charge burst: Bench Guardian never got Charge.

Heat on cold into Steam  [steam]
Relationship: Burn on a chilled enemy, or Chill on a burning one, cancels both and gives Steam to whoever did it. Dragon Oil spends Steam as a lance through Guard. Void Ink spends it as Guard for all.
Trade-off: The Burn and the Chill are both gone. Nothing burns and the enemy is not slowed.
Another approach: Against a line with no Guard to pierce, leaving the Chill on to weaken their strikes can be worth more than the lance.

  First recipe: Bronze + Frost Bloom + Dragon Oil sword, a Guardian, alone
    Against an armoured line. WON after 4 rounds, enemy health left 0/94, party health left 41/46.
    FIRED Bronze rings back x1: Cinder-knight: Ember guard. > Bench Guardian's Guard breaks. > Bench Guardian's Bronze rings back hits Cinder-knight for 5.
    FIRED Rime x4: Bench Guardian: Hold the line. > Bench Guardian hits Cinder-knight for 16. > Bench Guardian's Rime puts Chill on Cinder-knight.
    FIRED Dragon oil x8: Bench Guardian: Hold the line. > Bench Guardian hits Cinder-knight for 16. > Bench Guardian's Rime puts Chill on Cinder-knight. > Bench Guardian's Dragon oil boils off Chill on Cinder-knight.
    FIRED Steam lance x10: Bench Guardian's Rime puts Chill on Cinder-knight. > Bench Guardian's Dragon oil boils off Chill on Cinder-knight. > Bench Guardian's Dragon oil stores Steam (1). > Bench Guardian's Steam lance spends 1 Steam.

    FIRED Void ink x2: Bench Duelist: Double cut. > Bench Duelist's Void ink takes 1 Burn from Ashclaw brute.
    FIRED Void ink (Guard) x2: Bench Duelist: Double cut. > Bench Duelist's Void ink takes 1 Burn from Ashclaw brute. > Bench Duelist's Void ink (Guard) gives Bench Battlemage 1 Guard.
    FIRED Steam veil x4: Bench Duelist hits Ashclaw brute for 6. > Bench Duelist's Ember boils off Chill on Ashclaw brute. > Bench Duelist's Ember stores Steam (1). > Bench Duelist's Steam veil spends 1 Steam.

  Without its enabler: Iron + Ember Resin + Dragon Oil sword, alone: fire with no cold to meet
    Against an armoured line. TIMED_OUT after 8 rounds, enemy health left 15/94, party health left 24/46.
    FIRED Iron holds x1: Round 1. > Bench Guardian's Iron holds gives Bench Guardian 3 Guard.
    FIRED Ember x8: Bench Guardian: Hold the line. > Bench Guardian hits Cinder-knight for 8. > Bench Guardian's Ember puts Burn on Cinder-knight.
    FIRED Dragon oil x8: Bench Guardian: Hold the line. > Bench Guardian hits Cinder-knight for 8. > Bench Guardian's Ember puts Burn on Cinder-knight. > Bench Guardian's Dragon oil puts Burn on Cinder-knight (2).

Burn into Guard  [burn_guard]
Relationship: After its wielder acts, Void Ink takes Burn off the enemy the wielder faces and gives it to your weakest ally as Guard.
Trade-off: That Burn no longer deals damage. One Burn is one Guard, so it wants more fire than a single ember.
Another approach: Against a line that does not hit hard, the same fire with Dragon Oil burns every enemy and ends the fight sooner.

  First recipe: Iron + Ember Resin + Void Ink sword, a Guardian, alone: its own ember becomes its Guard

A cracked weapon pays  [salvage]
Relationship: A weapon that cracks leaves Scrap and wakes the rules that wait for a crack. Obsidian cracks by its nature, so the crack is a recipe and not luck.
Trade-off: A cracked weapon needs a repair after the fight, and the crack itself does nothing unless something answers it.
Another approach: With no Salvage Bell and no Runestone in the party, obsidian's crack is only a repair bill.

  First recipe: Obsidian + Ember Resin sword, a Guardian, alone, the Salvage Bell with the party

Marked for the kill  [marks]
Relationship: The party aims at a marked enemy and the hit that spends the Mark lands 3 harder. Starsteel adds 3 more when the wielder's own action spends it.
Trade-off: One Mark at a time, and whoever strikes next spends it. An ally, a riposte or a second arrow can take it first.
Another approach: With no Mark in the party, starsteel is only a costly metal. Moonsteel or obsidian does more for the gold.

  First recipe: Starsteel + Sun Ash sword, a Battlemage, alone: the opening sigil Marks, the bolt spends it, and noon light Marks again

    FIRED Noon light x3: Bench Battlemage: Opening sigil. > Bench Battlemage hits Cinder-knight for 14. > Bench Battlemage's Noon light puts Mark on Cinder-knight.

Cold to break  [shatter]
Relationship: Chill on an enemy is something to use. Moonsteel hits a chilled enemy for 3 more. A heavy frost weapon shatters the Chill it has just laid for 6.
Trade-off: The enemy's own strike spends Chill. Moonsteel needs it there before the hit, which takes a second cut or a second source.
Another approach: Shattering removes the Chill, so the enemy strikes at full strength. Against one hard hitter, leaving it on can be better.

  First recipe: Moonsteel + Frost Bloom sword, a Duelist, alone: the second cut finds the first cut's Chill

    FIRED Rime x2: Bench Duelist: Double cut. > Bench Duelist hits Ashclaw brute for 13. > Bench Duelist's Rime puts Chill on Ashclaw brute.
