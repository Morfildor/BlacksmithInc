Assets/Relics
Workshop relic icons: run-long relics shown in the Workshop relics row on the Shop and in the relic choice.

File rules: PNG with a transparent background unless the piece is a full scene or a framed portrait tile.
Name each file <id>.png using the IDs below, or drop a sheet plus a manifest.json that names its cells.
Any resolution is fine if it is a clean multiple of the listed in-game size; native pixel size is best.

Status as of 2026-10-10: nothing delivered yet. New with the visitors update: not in the game yet, optional. The relics are shown by name and text today.
Planned IDs (5), with the planned in-game size. Keep them apart from the blessing icons at a glance: a relic is an
object that stays in the workshop for the whole run, a blessing is a five-day favour.
  relic_salvagers_crucible                48x48   a small crucible that gives a melted blade's augment back
  relic_tempering_ledger                  48x48   a smith's ledger: rewards forging a different weapon family each time
  relic_collectors_seal                   48x48   a wax seal or signet: costly shelf sales earn seals
  relic_ashen_bellows                     48x48   ash-grey bellows: one forge a day burns tomorrow's strength
  relic_slot_empty                        48x48   an empty place on the relic shelf (the workshop holds three)
