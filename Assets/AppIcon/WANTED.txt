Assets/AppIcon
Launcher icon: one square image at 1024x1024 (foreground subject inside the central two thirds, so it survives the round mask), plus an optional separate background layer.
PRIORITY 1 (most urgent): needed for the store listing; the launcher icon is a placeholder. One piece.

File rules: PNG with a transparent background unless the piece is a full scene or a framed portrait tile.
Name each file <id>.png using the IDs below, or drop a sheet plus a manifest.json that names its cells.
Any resolution is fine if it is a clean multiple of the listed in-game size; native pixel size is best.

Status as of 2026-10-17: delivered with Retro Diffusion and in the game: ic_launcher_foreground_1024.png and
ic_launcher_background_1024.png (the adaptive layers, subject inside the 66 dp safe zone), ic_launcher_1024.png and
ic_launcher_512.png (the listing icon). The mipmaps in app/src/main/res are made from them.
