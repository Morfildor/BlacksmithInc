Assets/Heroes
Hero and townsfolk portraits, and the small hero battle frames.
PRIORITY 3 (new, living heroes): faces for the 6 peoples that have none, then a second face for the thin ones. PRIORITY 4: the 20 small battle frames (16x16). PRIORITY 5 (optional): the 6 visitor faces. More wardens: low.

File rules: PNG with a transparent background unless the piece is a full scene or a framed portrait tile.
Name each file <id>.png using the IDs below, or drop a sheet plus a manifest.json that names its cells.
Any resolution is fine if it is a clean multiple of the listed in-game size; native pixel size is best.

NEW WITH THE LIVING HEROES UPDATE (2026-10-17): FACES BY PEOPLE
  Heroes are now generated as one of 12 peoples (docs/Tiny_Blacksmith_Peoples_Names_and_Siege_Bestiary.md, section 3 for the look of
  each, section 15.2 for the brief). A hero wears a face tagged with their people; where no face is tagged, the game draws the
  neutral silhouette of their class (silhouette_guardian, silhouette_ranger, silhouette_duelist, silhouette_battlemage,
  silhouette_warden) with the right race in the text. Nothing breaks without new art; a face simply replaces the silhouette.
  Same format as the 20 delivered portraits: 64x64 framed tile, a base face and an upgraded face, no names or words in the art.
  Every face is worn by one class (guardian, ranger, duelist, battlemage or warden): say which in the manifest.json that
  comes with the sheet (as Implemented/heroes/manifest.json does), together with the people. Dress the class, not the people.
