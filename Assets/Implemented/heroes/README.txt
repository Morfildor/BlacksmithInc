Tiny Blacksmith - hero portrait set (v2, 2026-10-17)

66 heroes, each with 2 portrait states:
- base
- upgraded (the earned look after a title or 5 kills: a glow or trim on top of the same face)

Naming format:
hero_XX_class_base.png
hero_XX_class_upgraded.png

Counts:
- 66 heroes (one or more for every people and class)
- 132 PNG files, all native 64x64, opaque

All faces are one painterly style generated with Retro Diffusion (RD Pro painterly for the base, RD Pro edit for the
setting and the upgraded look), approved by the owner. Each has its own pose, colours and softly blurred setting.
manifest.json lists the class and the people (race_concept) of each; the game's tags are LaunchContent.portraitRaces.
Recipe, format standard and the per-hero matrix: docs/HERO_FACES_V2_PLAN.md.
